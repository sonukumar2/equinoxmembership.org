# Research notes — Equinox facts used on the site

Reviewed: November 2026. Every figure lives in `src/data/site.ts` (`facts`).

Equinox publishes **no national rate card**. Prices are quoted club by club, so the site presents reported ranges and says so.

## From Equinox's own documents (confirmed)

| Fact | Value on site | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commitment | Initial 12-month period, then month to month | equinox.com/join/terms (membership agreement §1.2–1.3) |
| Cancel notice | 30 days' written notice | Agreement §3.3 |
| Cancel methods | In club, mail to home club/Concierge, Cancellations@Equinox.com, online if joined online; phone only after initial period | Agreement §3.4; equinox.com/faq |
| Three-day right | Full refund before midnight of 3rd operating day | Agreement §3.1 |
| Early exit reasons | Disability, death, moving 25+ miles | Agreement §3.2 |
| Price changes | 30 days' notice after initial period | Agreement §2.1 |
| Initiation fee | Exists, one-time, due at signing (amount not stated) | Agreement §2.1 |
| Returned payment | $20 | Agreement §2.6 |
| Freeze | Up to 2 months, longer if medical; non-medical has a fee; no retroactive freezes; not for corporate/payroll | Agreement §1.4; equinox.com/member-policies |
| Guests | 2 passes for new members, replenish yearly; guest 16+, ID, waiver; 16–17 only with parent | equinox.com/faq; member-policies |
| Club count | 118 clubs (US, Canada, UK), ~50 in pipeline | Equinox Group CEO press release, via healthclubmanagement.co.uk |
